Keys and commands

F5 plays (the selection if there is one, otherwise all the text), F6 pauses and resumes, F7 stops. A change to the voice, rate or volume applies the next time you play.

Text goes straight to the engine, so commands work, as in DECtalk:

  • [:vs], [:vb], [:vg], [:vd], [:vt] change to Steve, Brittany, Gary, David or Taylor.
  • [:speed 300] sets words per minute, 30 to 1920; [:volume 50] the volume.
  • [:cv pi 150 hs 110 br 40] customizes pitch, head size, breathiness and more.
  • [:phone klattara enable] lets you write sounds in ARPAbet, such as [HH AH0 L OW1], and sing notes: [AA1<400,20>] sings note 20 for 400 ms.
  • [:reset] goes back to how the engine starts.

Klattara is open source: github.com/dgomez42/Klattara.
